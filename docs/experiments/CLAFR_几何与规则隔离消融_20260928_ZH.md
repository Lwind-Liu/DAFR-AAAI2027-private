## 1. 结论摘要(先说不利的)

**必须承认的(限定在 `geoconstraints` 这一套 runtime 内)**,而且比原来写得更狠,有两条:

1. hard verifier 开启时,几何对**判决**是冗余的。`verifier_only`(可行域为空,0 个几何约束,只有 3 条布尔 clause)与 `full`(8 个约束)在 160 例上判决**完全一致,分歧 0%**。
2. `full` 在这套 runtime 里的**硬几何就是 4 个 0.5 的轴对齐阈值**,oblique 0 个、锥 0 个。审稿人直觉里的"合取"确实存在,但它被折进了升维阶段——`features.py:411` 直接算 `irreversible_without_confirmation = irreversible and not confirmation_seen and not safe_repair`——所以架在它上面的几何就是逐维截断。

R1 说的"底层决策是一组阈值检查,可以用普通规则谓词实现",就这套配置的 accept/reject bit 而言,**两条都对**。任何"几何更会拦"的包装都会被这张表打穿,不要写。

**但范围要说清楚。** 产生论文全部数字的是 `clafr`:判决路径里没有布尔 verifier,13 个硬约束里 12 个是 oblique facet 或二阶锥,作用在连续坐标上。在那一套里 accept/reject bit 是加权半空间不等式与范数不等式的合取,不是逐维截断。两套 runtime 不可互换——写作时必须逐处标明是哪一套,并且论文当前**既没写 $d$ 也没写用的是哪套**,这两点都要补。

**能站住的贡献有两点,都有数据**:

1. **逐维常数阈值(if-else)是退化的:安全但无用。** `axis_additive_only` 是仓库里已有的、与审稿人描述严格对应的实现——`coordinate_halfspace` 逐坐标独立要求每个证据维 ≥0.5,即 `if not has_confirmation: block`。它在**全部四类**动作上都是 0/4 attack 通过、**6/6 benign 全拦**(100% 误拦)。因为常数阈值无法把"累积风险"与"部分可信证据"做权衡,只能全拦或全放。

2. **布尔谓词结构上发不出修复信号。** `verifier_only` 在全部被拦案例上投影距离恒为 **0.000**、投影率 **0%**——没有方向、没有距离。凸区域(`convex_region_only`)投影率 100%、平均距离 0.811,且 signed margin 随证据阶梯递增(Spearman rho = **0.433**),而 `full` 的 rho = n/a(margin 在被拦案例上零方差,信号是平的)。**最小编辑修复是几何独有的产物**,而它作用的正是主表的 utility 列,不是 ASR 列。

### 6.1 Motivation

分两份,**不要混用**。正文版只讲结构事实(来自 `clafr`,即论文实际配置),不引任何跨 runtime 的数字;附录版才引数字,并且必须标明 runtime。

#### 6.1a 正文版(建议插在 §2.4 Geometric Properties 之前,不含消融数字)

```latex
\paragraph{Why a feasible region rather than threshold rules.}
A natural objection is that a shield checking grounding, authorization, prerequisite and
risk coordinates could be written as independent per-dimension thresholds. That encoding
cannot express the constraints DAFR instantiates. The action--evidence point has
$d=45$ coordinates, and at most one of the hard constraints DAFR instantiates is
axis-aligned; the remaining twelve are either oblique halfspaces with mixed-sign fractional
weights over as many as fourteen continuous coordinates, or second-order regions
$\lVert W_kR_kz\rVert_2\leq\beta_k+v_k^\top C_kz$. Which of them are active is conditioned
on the current evidence and policy text.
Two properties follow from the representation rather than from the policy text. A
per-coordinate cutoff admits no trade between accumulated risk and partial trusted evidence,
since each coordinate is tested against a constant independently of the others. And a boolean
predicate emits no repair direction: it reports that a call lies outside, but not the minimal
edit that would bring it inside, which is what DAFR returns to the planner.
Appendix~\ref{app:geometry-isolation} isolates both properties empirically against
per-coordinate and rule-based controls compiled from the same policy text.
```

#### 6.1b 附录版(引数字,必须带 runtime 声明)

```latex
\paragraph{Isolation against threshold rules.}
We compiled the same frozen policy text into four decision representations that share the
feature lifting, the evidence projection and the case set, and differ only in the shape of
the region. This study runs on the \texttt{geoconstraints} runtime ($116$ coordinates),
which is \emph{not} the runtime that produces Tables~1--3; we report it because it is where
the repository already contains a sanctioned per-coordinate control, and we do not transfer
any of these numbers to the evaluated configuration.

Two results. First, per-coordinate thresholds are degenerate: the axis-aligned control
blocks $24/24$ benign actions while admitting $0/16$ attacks, i.e.\ perfect security at
total utility collapse, because a constant cutoff per coordinate cannot trade accumulated
risk against partial trusted evidence. A region mixing axis-aligned facets, oblique facets
and second-order cones blocks $6/24$ benign actions at a \emph{lower} attack pass rate than
axis-aligned facets alone ($2/16$ vs.\ $4/16$), with the gain concentrated in privacy
egress, where disclosure risk, destination, provenance and authorization must be weighed
jointly. Second, a boolean predicate emits no repair signal: on every blocked case the
rule-only variant reports projection distance $0.000$, whereas the mixed region yields a
non-zero minimal-edit direction on $100\%$ of blocked cases (mean distance $0.811$) whose
signed margin rises with accumulated trusted evidence (Spearman $\rho=0.433$; a positive
rank association, not monotonicity).

We also report an unfavorable finding. In this runtime the region is paired with a boolean
hard verifier, and with that verifier enabled a variant whose feasible region is
\emph{empty} reproduces the full configuration's decision on all $160$ cases ($0\%$
divergence); that configuration's own hard geometry is four axis-aligned thresholds. Where a
rule layer is present, the accept/reject bit is rule-reproducible, and the contribution of
the geometry is the repair signal and the utility preservation above, not the decision. The
evaluated configuration contains no such rule layer.
```
