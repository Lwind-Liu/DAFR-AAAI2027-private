## Transcription note / 转录说明

- The English text is transcribed from the screenshots provided in the project conversation. Headings, numbering, mathematical symbols, ratings, and confidence values are retained where visible.
- Minor display-only line wrapping was removed. Reviewer identities were not inferred beyond the labels visible in the screenshots.
- The AI-review screenshots do not show a rating or confidence field; none is invented here.
- Before using these files as an official archival record, one author should compare them once against the live review system or an exported review PDF/HTML.

- 英文内容依据项目对话中提供的截图转录；截图中可见的标题、编号、数学符号、评分和置信度均予以保留。
- 仅去除了网页显示造成的换行；未推断截图之外的审稿人身份。
- AI 审稿截图未显示评分或置信度，因此本目录不补写任何数值。
- 若要作为正式存档，建议由一位作者再与评审系统页面或导出的 PDF/HTML 逐字核对一次。
